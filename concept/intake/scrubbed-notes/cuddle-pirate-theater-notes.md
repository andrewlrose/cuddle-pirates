# Cuddle Pirate Theater — Original Concept Notes

## Core Concept

The Cuddle Pirates are a merry band of plushie pirates that commandeer bedtime
and put on a show to tell their bedtime stories, and then when everyone is
asleep, they steal all the comfiest pillows and softest blankies in all the
world, to take their fluffy plunder back to Puff-tooga, their Cuddle Pirate
Island.

One night, they swoop in on little Juniper. While dodging Mom so they remain
invisible, they entertain her with an acrobatic ragdoll antics show. With Mom
distracted across the hall doing laundry, Juniper admits to the Cuddles that
she is sad about her missing sock, and the Cuddle Pirates tell her a bedtime
story.

## Alternate Story Hooks

- alternate story: the Cuddle Pirates are like the tooth fairy; crying children
  are their Moby Dick and they must engage the beast to get him to sleep

- Story: Ava and the Razor Monster (or vacuum monster)

## The Ship

**The Fluffy Dutchman** — the Pirates' ship, which also serves as a pillowcase
for all of them. Their legs stick out the bottom so they can run with it when
they need to escape.

## Character Design Note

Character designs could be based on caricatures of Raylan's uncles and grandpas.
