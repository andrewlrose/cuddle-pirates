### Synopsis: Dreams from Antiquity (Morpheus)

The Cuddle Pirates receive a distress signal from a little girl named Lily,
experiencing strange and vivid dreams every night. Upon arriving in Lily's
bedroom, they discover her dreams are being affected by Morpheus — a
mischievous dream spirit who is stealing her dreams to manipulate them.

Remembering an ancient theory about dreams, the Cuddle Pirates venture into
the dream world: a labyrinthine landscape. They fend off Morpheus's minions
and reach the dream spirit's lair, where they discover Morpheus is lonely
and craves attention. They convince him to use his powers for positive,
inspiring dreams instead.

With Morpheus's help, the Cuddle Pirates guide Lily through wonderful dreams
that inspire bravery, kindness, and adventure.
